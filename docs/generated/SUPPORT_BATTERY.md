# Supports and tanks in every party (generated)

Generated by `dtcli support-battery`. Do not edit. Balance variant, 40 runs a cell, the same seeds for every role. Each support and tank class of a stage (sheet ClassKinds; one role for each class, the first of RoleOrder) in the last place of each party of the sheet BatteryParties, against the same party with the attacker in that place (the control). Contexts: boss, the fights of the checkpoint with everything full; walk, a row of 10 random battles of its floor; walk_boss, that row and then the boss. HP, TP and items are carried from fight to fight and nothing is healed between them but what the game heals (Generous Heart). Section support_battery of data_tables/measure_config.json.

Each figure is the mean over the cells of the role minus the control in the same cell: wins and reached (the share of rows that reach the boss) in points, length of the won fights in %, lowest HP, HP and TP left in points of the party's maximum, deaths, items and healing a run, damage taken in % of the control's. A cell serves: wins +8%, or wins not down that much and lowest HP +5%, or 0.3 deaths fewer, or 0.5 items fewer, and none of those four worse by as much (worse: one of them is). Serves as support: 25% or more of its cells serve; niche: some do; does not serve: none.
