Members and summon: their damage per unit of time with her against without her. Wins: the change of the share of fights won. Won fights shorter: the length of the won fights only, at the bosses where both sides won 20 fights or more. Gold a battle: the price of her stage over the battles won of her contract (boss fights do not count; the mean of her cells). Points per 100 gold: wins plus won fights shorter, in points, for each 100 gold a battle she costs: what she gives for her price when she walks a dungeon. Her actions: the share of each.
