Gold a battle: the price of her stage over the battles won of her contract (boss fights do not count; the mean of her cells). Points per 100 gold: wins plus won fights shorter, in points, for each 100 gold a battle she costs: what she gives for her price when she walks a dungeon. Her actions: the share of each.
