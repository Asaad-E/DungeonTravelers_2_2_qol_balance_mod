## Crown Resets

A character who reaches Level 30 or higher can undergo a Crown Reset. The character's level returns to Level 1, previously allocated skill points are refunded to be re-earned through leveling, and the character gains permanent bonus stat points. Resets stack additively up to a maximum cap of +50 total bonus stat points.

| Reset Level Range | Bonus Stat Points | Reset Level | Cumulative Cap |
| :--- | ---: | :--- | :--- |
| Level 99 | +10 | Level 1 | +50 stats max |
| Level 90–98 | +9 | Level 1 | +50 stats max |
| Level 80–89 | +8 | Level 1 | +50 stats max |
| Level 70–79 | +7 | Level 1 | +50 stats max |
| Level 60–69 | +6 | Level 1 | +50 stats max |
| Level 50–59 | +5 | Level 1 | +50 stats max |
| Level 40–49 | +4 | Level 1 | +50 stats max |
| Level 30–39 | +3 | Level 1 | +50 stats max |
