# Credits and sources

The game is *Dungeon Travelers 2-2: The Fallen Maidens & the Book of Beginnings*, by Aquaplus and Sting. This mod
holds no file of the game: it is built on each player's machine from that player's own copy.

## Where the figures come from

In this order, as `philosophy.md` sets it:

1. **The game itself.** Its data files and its program are the source of every value and every formula
   (`docs/FORMULAS.md` gives the address of each).
2. **The guides of the players**, for what the game does not store, and to know what players find wrong. Every figure
   taken from a guide names it in the workbook that holds it, and every complaint of the register
   (`data_tables/balance_criteria.xlsx`, sheet `Criticisms`) names the page it comes from.

## The guides

- **The Japanese wiki of the game** (atwiki, `w.atwiki.jp/dt2-2`). Its editors documented the game more thoroughly
  than anyone: the characters and their unique skills, the party skills, the enchantments, the bosses of the story
  and of the postgame with the level and the preparation each asks for. Most of the complaints the Balance variant
  answers were first written there, and the difficulty of the postgame is held to what those pages tell.
- **The GameFAQs walkthrough and guide of Dungeon Travelers 2-2**, by Hentaicheg
  (`gamefaqs.gamespot.com`, guide 80834): the review of every class and skill, of the unique skills, the party
  skills, the mercenaries and the grand sealbooks, and the walkthrough of each dungeon with its recommended levels.
  Its criticisms are the second half of the register.
- **The GameFAQs FAQ of the first Dungeon Travelers 2**, by DoopDoop (guide 72905), for the unique skills the girls
  had in the first game.

Thanks to all of them. Where this mod says the game does something else than a guide says, the program was read, and
the reading is in `docs/FORMULAS.md` for anyone to check.

## Method

The way of working (two variants, the game before the guides, nothing written in code, a build that refuses a text
it cannot prove) is set out in `philosophy.md`; it was first worked out by the same author on an earlier project.
