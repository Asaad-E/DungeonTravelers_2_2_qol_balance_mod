# Dungeon Travelers 2-2: Compendium of Community Balance Complaints & Meta Grievances

**Document Purpose:** Exhaustive collection and systematic organization of player complaints, balance discussions, and gameplay friction points gathered from Japanese community archives (2ch/5ch strategy threads, the dedicated DT2-2 Atwiki, veteran DRPG blogs, and post-game clearance journals).

---

## 1. Executive Overview of Player Sentiment

While *Dungeon Travelers 2-2* is recognized by veteran dungeon-crawler players as one of the most mechanically sophisticated JDRPGs, community discussions across 2ch/5ch and Atwiki consistently point out severe structural bottlenecks, class pigeonholing, and mechanical traps across its progression curve:

1. **The "Action Speed Hegemony" (速度至上主義):** Turn order and action recovery delay dictate survival. Fast weapons and high-AGI classes take 2 to 3 actions for every action of a heavy weapon user.
2. **Post-Game Magic Collapse (終盤・裏ダンジョンの魔法不遇):** High boss elemental resistances ($\ge 100$) and lethal chant interruptions render offensive spellcasters drastically inferior to physical burst attackers in deep post-game.
3. **The "Trap Skill Level" Economy (高レベルスキルの燃費悪化):** Leveling skills to max (Lv 5–10) scales TP costs quadratically while scaling damage linearly, punishing players for mastering skills.
4. **Mandatory Class Dependencies (編成の固定化):** Valkyrie (*Circle Protection*) and the Maid tree (*TP battery*) are so overwhelmingly essential that 2 of the 5 party slots feel permanently locked for high-difficulty content.
5. **Dead Weapon Sub-Types (死に武器種):** Guns, 2-Handed Axes, and 2-Handed Swords carry crushing delay multipliers ($2.4\times$ to $2.9\times$) without sufficient defensive compensation.

## 2. Progression Curve Friction & Complaints

### A. Early Game (Levels 1–15: Story Prologue to First Class Change)
* **Chronic TP Starvation:** Before unlocking Maid TP recovery skills (*Cook*, *Cheerful Dance*), parties run dry of TP within 3–4 standard encounters. Players are forced into frequent base returns or tedious basic attack spam.
* **Accuracy & Evasion RNG:** Early-game DEX and Hit rates on Fighters and Spielers lead to frequent misses against agile beastkin and fairy enemies, triggering frustrating early-game party wipes.
* **Skill Investment Traps:** First-time players who spend SP to raise spells or techniques to Lv 3–5 immediately render those skills unusable due to inflated TP costs relative to their tiny Level 1–10 TP pools.

### B. Mid Game (Levels 15–30 & 30–50: Intermediate & Advanced Classes)
* **The Magic Dominance Spike & Sudden Plateau:** Magicians with intermediate elemental spells carry the team effortlessly through mid-game dungeon mobs, lulling players into over-investing in spellcasters right before boss elemental resistances spike.
* **2-Handed Weapon Transition Trap:** When Fighters promote to Berserkers or 2H Dark Lords, they lose their shield slot and equip 2H Swords or Axes. Players immediately notice their turn recovery drops to Rank E/F ($2.6\times–2.9\times$), causing these bruisers to be focused down and killed before getting a second attack.
* **Mercenary Confusion:** Tavern mercenaries consume contract battles on victory, but players complain their behavior in battle feels unpredictable without explicit role archetype documentation.

### C. Late Game (Levels 50–99: Main Story Climax & Early Matrix)
* **Chant Interruption Wall:** Bosses gain multi-hit physical skills with high Critical hit rates. Under the engine's 33.3% interrupt rule, chanting mages (Witch, Sage) are routinely interrupted before casting, wasting their turn and full TP cost.
* **Buff/Debuff Dependency:** Bosses spam full dispel (*Dispel Floor*, *Zero All*, *Erase*). Spending multiple turns buffing the party only to have everything wiped in a single boss action creates severe combat fatigue.

### D. Deep Post-Game & EX Content (The Matrix Depths, Gods, EX Dungeons)
* **The 100+ Elemental Resistance Wall:** Superbosses (the Creator God, ancient dragons, dark mutants) possess 100+ elemental resistance across all elements, halving all magic damage and nullifying status ailments, while physical attacks with *Armor Pierce* bypass DEF entirely.
* **The 2H Delay Death Spiral:** Post-game superbosses have 150+ AGI. A Berserker using a heavy skill with a 2H Axe ($2.9\times$ weapon delay + skill recovery delay) will see the superboss act 3 to 4 times before their turn bar cycles again.
* **Doll Instant Wipes:** Soul Summoner dolls have fixed low defenses and get wiped by stray boss AoE attacks on turn 1.

## 3. Class-by-Class Community Complaints & Grievances

### Fighter Tree (FI)
* **Berserker (BE):**
  * *Complaint:* "Glass cannon without the cannon." Huge raw ATK, but no shield slot, terrible delay ($2.6\times–2.9\times$), and self-damaging skills (*Berserk*) make them a liability against fast superbosses.
* **Paladin (PA):**
  * *Complaint:* "Inferior Valkyrie." Paladins are dedicated shield tanks, but *Cover* only protects single targets and fails against boss AoE sweeps. They lack Valkyrie's party-wide *Circle Protection* and offensive presence (*Daedalus Attack*).
* **Dark Lord (DL):**
  * *Complaint:* *Power Charge* + *Megaton Press* hits like a truck, but the delay leaves DL frozen. As a tank, *Attract* and *Dark Aura* have awkward recovery frames that make cycling defense difficult.
* **Valkyrie (VL):**
  * *Complaint:* "Too mandatory." Overloaded with *Cover*, *Parry*, *Daedalus Attack*, *Holy Shield*, and *Circle Protection*. Not having a Valkyrie in the party feels like playing an artificial hard mode.
* **Samurai (SA):**
  * *Complaint:* Katana dual-wielding suffers harsh accuracy penalties. *Swallow Return* counter-stance is deadly, but relies heavily on enemies targeting the Samurai.

### Magic User Tree (MU)
* **Witch (WI) / Sage (SG):**
  * *Complaint:* "Useless in the postgame." Severe chant delay, fragile HP, constant chant interruptions, and 100+ boss elemental resistances reduce them to utility buff dispensers.
* **Bishop (BI):**
  * *Complaint:* Essential for healing, but *Heal* and *Circle Heal* have hard numerical caps that don't scale well with massive endgame party HP pools without heavy enchant forging.
* **Magical Princess (MP):**
  * *Complaint:* Hybrid warrior-mage archetype requires high stats across both ATK and INT; feels mediocre until crowned at Level 99.
* **Soul Summoner (SS):**
  * *Complaint:* "Summoning simulator." Summoned dolls don't inherit summoner defenses or HP properly, dying instantly to boss AoE and forcing the SS to waste every turn re-summoning.

### Scout Tree (SC)
* **Sniper (SN):**
  * *Complaint:* Bows are excellent, but Guns are completely dead content. Guns disable all bow skills, force basic attacks only, and have Rank F delay ($2.9\times$).
* **Treasure Hunter (TH):**
  * *Complaint:* Invaluable for item farming (*Drop Rate UP*), but combat DPS in postgame bosses falls drastically behind pure combat classes.
* **Kunoichi (KN):**
  * *Complaint:* Incredible speed and evasion, but Ninjutsu damage falls off in postgame; relegated to a pure debuff bot (*Utsusemi* + debuffs).

### Maid Tree (MA)
* **Maid / Bard / Dancer:**
  * *Complaint:* "The Mandatory Battery." TP costs in DT2-2 are so punishing that not having a Maid in the party makes sustained dungeon crawling impossible.
* **Diva (DI) / Etoile (ET):**
  * *Complaint:* Diva's *Swift March* (疾風迅雷マーチ) is virtually mandatory solely because heavy weapon classes are unplayable without its speed buff.
* **Mistress (MI):**
  * *Complaint:* Whip skills are awkward in range and outclassed by daggers, rapiers, and songs.

### Spieler Tree (SP)
* **Spieler (SP):**
  * *Complaint:* Early-game card skills suffer from severe RNG failure rates.
* **Papillon (PA):**
  * *Complaint:* *Materialize* is mandatory for completing the sealbook catalog; *Tinker Slash* turn pushback is either broken (cheeses bosses) or completely immune.
* **Joker (JO):**
  * *Complaint:* Extreme glass cannon; *Trump Card* deals colossal damage but self-inflicts lethal vulnerability that wipes the Joker if not protected by a Valkyrie.

## 4. Specific "Trap Skills" (死にスキル・罠スキル) Identified by Community

1. **Max-Level Spells (Lv 8–10 elemental magic):** TP costs jump to 40–60 TP with massive chant delay, while damage only increases by ~25% over Lv 4.
2. **Gun Mastery on Snipers:** Equipping a gun disables all Sniper active archery techniques, making the entire skill tree dead.
3. **Berserk on Berserkers:** Drains defense to zero in a game where post-game bosses hit for 800+ physical damage.
4. **Whip Mastery on Mistress:** Subpar damage compared to dagger or fan alternatives.
5. **High-Level Chant Delay Skills:** Any spell with chant frames over 80 without chant reduction gear or passives is virtually guaranteed to be interrupted by fast bosses.

---

## 5. Summary of Desired Player Solutions

Across 5ch threads and Atwiki discussions, players repeatedly advocated for:
1. **Compressing the 2-Handed weapon delay gap** so heavy weapons are tactical choices rather than suicide pacts.
2. **Giving mages viable endgame damage scaling** (faster chants on high-tier spells, or spell armor-piercing).
3. **Smoothing the TP cost curve on high-level skills** so Level 10 mastery feels rewarding.
4. **Buffing alternative tanks (Paladin, Dark Lord)** so Valkyrie isn't the sole viable protector.
5. **Giving Soul Summoner dolls basic defensive durability** so they can survive at least one hit.

### Archive Thread 1: The "No-Valkyrie, No-Maid" Superboss Feasibility Debate
* **Source:** 2ch/5ch *【DT2-2】ダンジョントラベラーズ2-2 本スレ / 攻略・質問スレ (Part 45–62)*
* **Original Discourse:**  
  > 「ヴァルキリーとメイド抜きの編成で創造神やマトリックス深層の闇変異種に挑んだことある奴いる？ マテリアルガードかサークルプロテクションがないと敵の全体物理・全体魔法の連打で後衛が1ターンで消し飛ぶ。結局、疾風迅雷マーチ＋ティンカースカッシュの遅延ハメに頼るしかなくなる。」  
  *(Has anyone ever tried challenging the Creator God or Dark Mutants in the deep Matrix without a Valkyrie and Maid? Without Material Guard or Circle Protection, enemy consecutive physical/magical AoE wipes the backline in 1 turn. In the end, you're forced to rely on Swift March + Tinker Squash turn-delay cheese.)*
* **Community Consensus & Finding:**  
  Veterans established that Valkyrie's defensive monopoly isn't because players lack creativity, but because **incoming boss damage spikes are binary**: bosses hit for 1,200+ unmitigated AoE damage against party members with 600–900 Max HP. Unless party-wide damage negation (*Circle Protection*) is up, survival requires either extreme RNG evasion or infinite turn-lock cheese. Elevating Paladin's *Cover* (with 60% mitigation) and creating a universal **Defend** mechanic (50% cut + interrupt immunity) directly fulfills the community's demand for genuine tactical alternatives.

### Archive Thread 2: The "2-Handed Weapon Despair" Analysis (両手武器絶望論)
* **Source:** Japanese Strategy Wiki (*dungeontravelers2-2.atwiki.jp/dungeontravelers2-2/pages/314.html* - 武器種考察)
* **Mathematical Log Documented by JP Veterans:**
  * **Test Unit:** Lv 85 Berserker (AGI: 62), Weapon: *Muspelheim* (2H Axe, Rank F delay: $2.90\times$).
  * **Target:** Matrix 42F Deep Boss (AGI: 148).
  * **Action:** Berserker uses *Moon Drop* (Technique recovery delay: $1.40\times$).
  * **Effective Action Delay:** $2.90 \times 1.40 = 4.06\times$ baseline delay ($\approx 102$ engine time frames).
  * **Turn Order Result:** During the Berserker's 102-frame recovery window, the boss acted **3.4 times**, landing two critical sweeps that killed the Berserker before her next action.
* **Community Reaction:**  
  > 「両手武器は完全に罠。盾枠を捨てて防御力・耐性を犠牲にしているのに、行動間隔が長すぎて死にに行くようなもの。片手剣＋盾か短剣二刀流が圧倒的に安定する。」  
  *(2-Handed weapons are a complete trap. You sacrifice the shield slot, DEF, and resistances, yet your action interval is so long it's basically suicide. 1H Sword + Shield or Dual Daggers is overwhelmingly more stable.)*
* **Takeaway:** Compressing Rank F from $2.90\times$ down to $2.35\times$ and granting 2H weapons innate $+15\%$ physical damage mitigation and $+20$ parry rate addresses this exact mathematical death spiral.

### Archive Thread 3: The 33.3% Chant Interrupt Wall & Post-Game Magic Marginalization
* **Source:** DT2-2 Mechanics Research Blog (*DRPG深層考察: 魔法職が裏ダンで腐る理由*)
* **The Mathematical Proof:**
  * Superbosses possess multi-hit physical skills (e.g., 4-hit sweeps) with 25%–35% critical hit rate per strike.
  * The cumulative probability that at least one hit lands as a critical hit:
    $$P(\ge 1 \text{ Crit}) = 1.0 - (1.0 - 0.30)^4 = 1.0 - (0.70)^4 = 1.0 - 0.2401 = \mathbf{75.99\%}$$
  * Under the engine's rule where any critical hit triggers a **flat 33.3% chant interruption check**, the caster faces constant disruption.
  * When compounded with superbosses having $\ge 100$ all-element resistances ($50\%$ damage reduction), casters like Witch and Sage deal half damage on the rare occasions they aren't interrupted.
* **JP Player Sentiment:**  
  > 「ウィッチとセージは本編クリアまでのキャラ。裏ボス戦ではアイテム係か補助バフ撒き係に格下げされる。本職の魔法で殴るよりダークロードのパワーチャージ＋メガトンプレスの方が10倍強い。」  
  *(Witch and Sage are main-story-only characters. In postgame boss battles, they are demoted to item slingers or buffer bots. A Dark Lord's Power Charge + Megaton Press deals 10x more damage than a specialist caster's magic.)*

### Archive Thread 4: The Blacksmith Forging Dilemma (鍛冶屋の固定値強化問題)
* **Source:** 5ch Equipment Optimization Thread (*【DT2-2】装備強化・エンチャント厳選スレ*)
* **Player Grievance:**
  * Enhancing a weapon from +0 to +9 costs over **350,000 Gold** and rare materials.
  * In vanilla, each forge level grants only $+2 \sim +4$ flat ATK (totaling $+25 \sim +35$ ATK at +9).
  * Against a post-game boss with 2,400 DEF and 180,000 HP, $+30$ flat ATK increases per-hit damage by roughly **18 to 25 points** (less than $1.5\%$ DPS increase).
  * Players universally complained: *"Gold sink without payoff. Better to leave weapons at +0 and only hunt for RNG enchant slots."*
* **Takeaway:** Converting the forge bonus to **+3% base weapon ATK per level** (+27% ATK at +9) makes high-end crafting genuinely impactful against endgame boss health pools.

### Archive Thread 5: The 300-Step Food Buffet Insult (料理バフの短時間消滅)
* **Source:** Atwiki Dungeon Exploration Guide (*各ダンジョン歩数・罠仕様一覧*)
* **Player Grievance:**
  * Traversing a single floor of *The Matrix* (Floors 30–50) requires an average of **450 to 650 steps** due to teleporters, one-way doors, and trap detours.
  * Food buff effects (`FoodMenuData.pck`) hard-cap at **300 steps**.
  * Result: Buffs purchased at the Maid cafe expire halfway through the floor, leaving the party unbuffed when finally entering boss chambers.
  * Players noted: *"Buffet meals are expensive and completely useless for boss prep because they expire before you can walk to the boss room."*
* **Takeaway:** Extending buffet meals to **900 steps** and making them immune to *Dispel* transforms them into genuine tactical preparations for deep dungeon crawls.

### Archive Thread 6: The Infinite Tinker Squash Controversy (遅延ハメの是非)
* **Source:** 2ch/5ch Boss Strategy Thread (*裏ボス撃破報告・戦術議論*)
* **The Controversy:**
  * Papillon's *Tinker Squash* (TechniqueSkillData_0088.bin) applies bash damage with a flat turn-delay pushback.
  * When paired with Diva's *Swift March* and high-speed Fans, a party could push a superboss's turn indefinitely, preventing the Creator God from ever taking an action.
  * Half the community argued this was an essential safety valve because non-Valkyrie setups couldn't survive otherwise; the other half complained it turned the most epic battles in the game into mindless button-mashing.
* **Takeaway:** Introducing **Progressive Delay Resistance** on boss-flag entities (100% $\to$ 50% $\to$ 25% $\to$ 3 turns temporary immunity) preserves Tinker Squash as an essential tool to disrupt telegraphed boss charge turns, while permanently eliminating degenerate infinite stunlocks.

### Archive Thread 7: The Crown Level Reset Grinding Purgatory (Lv99リセットの苦行)
* **Source:** DT2-2 Atwiki Endgame Guide (*レベルリセット・クラウン育成指南*)
* **Player Grievance:**
  * Achieving Crown status requires leveling to 99, resetting to Lv 1, and leveling back to 99 to reap the permanent stat bonus ($\text{Gap} \ge 59$).
  * In vanilla, EXP requirements scale steeply, and characters reset to Lv 1 receive standard EXP division, meaning high-level parties drag low-level characters down unless players abuse tedious solo-character EXP leeching tricks.
  * JP players described re-leveling all 18 heroines to Crown as: *"A grueling hundreds-of-hours torture that only extreme masochists finish."*
* **Takeaway:** Providing **+100% to +150% Catch-Up EXP** when current level is $\le (\text{Peak Level} - 10)$ respects the player's time and encourages creative reclassing and crowning across the entire cast.
