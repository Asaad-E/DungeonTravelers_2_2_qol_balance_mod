# Catching up and the crown: options compared (generated)

Generated by `tools/catch_up_options.py` from the sheet `CatchUpOptions` of `balance_criteria.xlsx`. Do not edit: change the sheet and run the tool again. Nothing here is applied to the game.

Random battles with 5 members alive, in the zone whose enemies are closest to the level of the party, with no other EXP bonus. Bosses, quests and what the party gains meanwhile are left out.

## What each option does to a party that is on level

"Own pace" is the battles one more level costs a member on level in its zone. "A zone above" is the EXP a battle gives a party that fights in the zone 7 and 12 levels above its own, as a multiple of what vanilla gives there: above 1 the option pays for running ahead of the curve.

The last column matters because the result of a battle handles EXP as 32-bit floats (`0x445964 - 0x445a29`): above 16,777,216 not every integer exists, so small gains would be rounded. Vanilla stays far below it.

## The crown

A crown is a reset bonus of 50 (`docs/FORMULAS.md` 10). Battles to raise one member from level 1 in the zone that gives most EXP (St15_1160, enemies around level 85), carried by four members who stay alive, times the resets of each recipe.
